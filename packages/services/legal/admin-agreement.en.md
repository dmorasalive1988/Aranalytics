# Publishing administration agreement — DRAFT v0.1

> Draft for legal review. Not the final version.

**The essentials, no fine print**

1. **Your songs are 100% yours.** Pluma doesn’t buy or take ownership of your songs. We only represent you to administer them.
2. **What Pluma does.** We register your songs with collecting societies and platforms through our administration agreement with Warner Chappell, collect the royalties they earn and pay you with an official statement.
3. **What Pluma charges.** An annual membership (Socio USD 20 or Pro USD 50) and a fee on what Pluma receives for your songs: 20% on Socio and 15% on Pro. The fee is the one for the plan you have on the date each statement is published.
4. **When you get paid.** On Warner Chappell’s payment calendar. We will never show you an estimate as if it were official.
5. **Splits.** We only administer the shares of Pluma members. Shares of co-writers who aren’t members are recorded for information only.
6. **Disputes.** If a song is in dispute, its royalties are held until it’s resolved.
7. **Expired membership.** You get a 15-day grace period. After that, the Network and catalogs are paused, but we keep administering your registered songs and paying you under this agreement.
8. **Leaving.** You can end the agreement with written notice. We help you transfer your catalog and settle royalties in progress.
9. **Your data.** We handle it under Colombia’s Law 1581 of 2012, Brazil’s LGPD and U.S. state laws such as the CCPA. You can ask for access, correction or deletion.
10. **Representations.** You confirm you wrote the songs you register and that you will tell us if a song was generated with artificial intelligence.

By signing, you accept this agreement and Pluma’s terms of use.
